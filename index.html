    FAQ

    Questions lawyers ask first

    Will you ever connect to my computer?

    No. We never ask for remote access, passwords or access codes. You install it yourself, or we install it in front of you on a video call or on site. For support, you share your screen; we only guide.

    Can it answer from the internet?

    No. Open web search is off by design. It answers only from the files you put in your corpus, and says so when it finds nothing.

    What can I put in the corpus?

    Your own contracts, templates, memos and past matters, plus open official law texts you are allowed to use. We don't include or copy commercial publisher content.

    Does it replace a lawyer?

    No. It finds and cites passages and prepares drafts. A qualified professional reviews every output.
